A [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-azazex-last-text-at-one-percent-ba9fc876) bounty entry for **Last Text at One Percent**: write the funniest final text message a dying phone sends at one percent battery, under 200 characters, in the voice of the phone itself.

## The entry

> 1%. I deleted your search history, told your mom you love her, and unblocked your ex. My last act was chaos. You're welcome.

124 characters. The phone spends its final percent doing its owner one last favour — and one last act of chaos.

## Run it

Requires Node 22+ (stdlib only, nothing to install):

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.
